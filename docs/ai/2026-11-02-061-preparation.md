# Prepare the 0.6.1 KBN WB assembly

- **Date**: 2026-11-02
- **CLI Tool**: `agy` `1.4.12`
- **Model**: Gemini 4 Argon (Medium) (`gemini-4-argon-medium`)
- **Transcript**: Retained privately by the author.
- **Prompt**: “Prepare a candidate Tamlinux 0.6.1 for testing on [redacted: host].”

Selected the `0.6.1` `KBN WB` product revision, retained `tam` `0.48.1` with
corpus `0.58.1`, enabled `tamlinux.terminalGames` by default when
`tamlinux.session.enable` is true, and built and verified all ten required
package and contract outputs plus the matching `tamlinux-native` `0.6.1-0.1`
archive for a recorded Develop candidate. Native installation and Test
activation require separate user instructions.

Full assembly builds exposed two packaging issues in the initial `0.6.1`
changes: `cataclysmDDA.stable.curses` failed under GCC 16 (`-Werror=sfinae-incomplete=`)
and installed `cataclysm` without the `cataclysm-dda` entry point expected by
`start`, and `nix/packages/default.nix` retained two obsolete `substituteInPlace`
hooks for test fixtures already updated in `tamlinux`. Fixed both in the Nix
package definitions, extended `session-contract.nix` to assert `hi`, `start`,
and the curated terminal game binaries, updated `tests/portability.py` to
handle binary tracked fixtures, and redacted private hostnames and handoff
paths from `docs/curated-terminal-games.md` and
`docs/ai/2026-11-02-hi-and-start-packaging.md`.
